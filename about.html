👋 Our Story

Hi! We're Kris and Jordynne Denty, the owners of Bounce Boss Inflatables. Jordynne was born and raised in Fort Saskatchewan, and today we're proud to be raising our own family in the same community we call home. We started Bounce Boss because we wanted to build something of our own while bringing more fun to the families around us. Our goal is simple: provide clean, safe, commercial-grade inflatables with the kind of service we'd be happy to give our own friends, family, and neighbours.
